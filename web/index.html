    WELCOME, SCULPTOR

    A little matter.
    A universe of possibilities.

    Place a world, choose its distance and speed, then press Run. Gravity takes care of the rest.

    • 100% speed starts a circular orbit. Above about 142%, a world can escape.
    • The green band marks potential habitability. The entire orbit must stay inside it.
    • Collisions combine worlds. Give neighbors space if you want them to survive.
    • Rewind returns to the initial setup and removes later placements. Undo removes the most recent edit and re-simulates. A fresh system clears everything.
    • Each challenge unlocks discoveries. The sandbox has all tools from the beginning.

    A playful physics model: sizes and collision radii are enlarged; formation is accelerated; habitability is simplified.
